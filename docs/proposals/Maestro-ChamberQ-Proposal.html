Your chamber with ChamberQ

Your chamber days fill up fast — families call for serials, the waiting area gets loud, and you spend energy on the queue instead of the consult.

ChamberQ is built for that: clearer serials, one calm board per sitting, and a portfolio site under your name.

1. Portfolio website under your name

Credentials, chambers, FAQ, and Book — so families find you, not a hospital template. We set up the first version; you edit when you want.

2. Online serial booking

Patients book a sitting and get a ticket link — no app, no password. Walk-ins and phone serials stay in the same queue.

3. Ticket + waiting-room TV

Live position on the phone; one outdoor screen per sitting calls the next numbers aloud.

4. Consult screen

Notes, vitals, medicines, and follow-up in one place. Your favourites are remembered; records stay under your name.

5. Prescription print / WhatsApp home

Print a slip or send a short link on WhatsApp — medicines and advice for the family; diagnosis stays in your consult record.

Patient path

From the family’s phone — no sign-up, just their mobile number.

Open your site → Book sitting → Get ticket → TV call → Consult + Rx

They can share the ticket, check live position, and get a notice if you mark the day late — without you repeating the same update at the desk.

At your desk

Live Queue Control — one screen for the whole sitting.

Start sitting → Call next → Complete visit → Next / switch sitting

Staff can help with arrivals and the outdoor TV without seeing clinical notes. Switch sitting when you move chamber — the queue and screen follow that location.

Full experience without sign-up — book, follow the ticket, check status by phone, receive prescription links. Soft launch beside phone serial; no big-bang day required.
