## A day at your chamber

One doctor, up to five chambers — same flow every sitting. Patients book or walk in; you run one sitting at a time from your desk.

**Patient path** (from the family’s phone — no sign-up): Open your site → book sitting → get ticket → TV call → consult + Rx. They can share the ticket, check live position, and get a notice if you mark the day late.

**At your desk** (Live Queue Control): Start sitting → call next → complete visit → next / switch sitting. Staff can help with arrivals and the outdoor TV without seeing clinical notes. When you move chamber, the queue and screen follow that location.

Full experience without sign-up — book, follow the ticket, check status by phone, receive prescription links. Soft launch beside phone serial; no big-bang day required.
