Business Proposal08

Home programmes
& what we build

Sending the plan home

  • Follow-up: “1 week”, “as needed”, or a date
  • Medicines from a searchable list — including “same as last visit”
  • Print a clean copy for the patient
  • WhatsApp / SMS link: medicines, advice, follow-up — not the full file

We set up your CBPH

  • Panchlaish clinic & female department room if separate
  • Your physiotherapists and logins
  • Morning / evening sittings with a daily cap you choose
  • Reception on Live Queue Control; one TV bookmark per sitting
  • Website copy and photos in your voice

Kept simple on purpose

Therapy packages (e.g. 10 sessions) stay how you sell them; each visit still takes its own serial. Patients do not create login accounts — ticket link and phone lookup are enough.
