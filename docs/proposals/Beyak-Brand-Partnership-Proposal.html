Section 1

Why your digital presence matters now

Most patients research a doctor online before booking. Your page is the modern waiting-room first impression.

1. Awareness

Target: Someone searching or referred

A patient searches “best skin specialist in Chattogram” — or hears your name from a friend — and looks you up online.

2. Verification

Target: Facebook, Instagram, Google

They check your page, Google Business Profile, ratings, and recent posts to judge credibility.

3. Trust-building

Target: The patient who is still unsure

Educational videos, patient-friendly explanations, and a clean, active profile reassure them you are knowledgeable and approachable.

4. Decision

Target: Choosing between two doctors

A polished, active presence tips the decision in your favor over a competing doctor with a weak or inactive profile.

If this journey is left unmanaged, even the most skilled doctor can lose patients to someone with a stronger online image. Beyak Branding exists to make sure that never happens to you.

What we will do for you

A complete, done-for-you brand management package — four pillars designed to build awareness, trust, and long-term patient confidence.

1. Page Maintenance & Management

Scheduled Facebook & Instagram posts (education, tips, behind-the-scenes), a content calendar around health days and seasonal concerns, community replies, and monthly reporting on reach, engagement, and growth.

2. Video Shoot & Edit

3. Static Design

Every visual is a silent introduction to your practice

4. Google My Business (GMB)

Where “near me” searches turn into real appointments

Profile setup and optimization, regular GMB posts, review generation with professional replies, and local SEO so you rank higher for searches like “doctor near me.”
