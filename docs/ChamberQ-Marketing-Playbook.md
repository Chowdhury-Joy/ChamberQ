# ChamberQ — Marketing & Sales Playbook

**Audience:** Marketing team, sales partners (marketers), anyone pitching ChamberQ  
**Product:** ChamberQ  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-14  

Use this as your one source of truth when talking to doctors, writing ads, or training new partners. Keep language simple. Sell the **calmer chamber**, not the software.

---

## 1. What we sell (in one breath)

ChamberQ gives a Bangladesh doctor (or clinic) a **branded patient website**, **online serial booking**, a **live waiting-room queue**, and a **simple staff panel** — so patients wait less, phones interrupt less, and the chamber feels organized.

**Patients do not pay online.** They book a serial, then pay at the chamber — same as today. That matches how chambers already work and removes payment friction.

**We set everything up for them.** There is no self-signup. Leads come through WhatsApp. We create their page and go live together.

---

## 2. The problem we solve (use this story)

**Before (typical solo chamber):**
- Phone rings during consults
- Patients wait ~2 hours with little information
- Scramble at the chamber door
- Weak word of mouth

**After (with ChamberQ):**
- Serials booked online
- Waits closer to ~15 minutes when the system is used well
- Calm live queue at the room
- Patients feel respected and recommend the doctor

**Real-life analogy:**  
Think of a busy bakery that used to shout numbers and leave people standing for hours. Now customers take a number on their phone, see roughly when to come back, and the counter calls the next person in order. Same bakery — less chaos.

**Core message:** *Give patients their time back.* When patients feel respected, they tell others. That is the growth engine.

## 3. Who to target

### Best fit — Maestro (featured, full one-doctor package)
- One doctor
- Practices at **1–5 chambers / hospital OPDs** on different days
- Frustrated by phone calls and long waits
- Wants website + booking + live queue + prescription together
- Comfortable with WhatsApp (almost everyone)

### Also sell — modules (same doctor, pick pieces)
- **Website + booking** only, or add **Prescription**, or add **Live queue**
- Prices under the Maestro card on the marketing site — not a separate brand
- **Rising Star is retired** as a named plan (no third product story)

### Best fit — Clinic plan
- Multi-doctor practice
- Needs lab tests on the booking flow
- Wants one system across many chambers / doctors

### Weak fit (for now)
- Hospitals that need full HIS / EMR / billing integration
- Buyers who insist on **online pre-payment** as a must-have for v1 (we do pay-at-chamber only; online pay is later-stage)
- Buyers who want to “build it themselves” with no onboarding call

## 5. How the sale works (journey)

1. Doctor lands on the marketing site (or your referral link).
2. They tap **WhatsApp** — that is the only primary CTA. No self-signup form.
3. You (or central sales) qualify Maestro vs modules vs Clinic, answer FAQs, collect chamber details.
4. **Super Admin** creates their tenant (URL like `chamberq.com/drkarim`), ticks modules and any launch offers, attaches your referral / discount if any, and hands over admin login.
5. We set up branding, sessions, and go live together.
6. Doctor pays setup → then monthly. Super Admin confirms payments in the system.

**Always push WhatsApp**, not “sign up on the website.” High-touch onboarding protects demo quality and fits how Bangladesh chamber buyers actually buy.

## 6. Partner / marketer commissions

If you are a sales partner:

| Commission type | Typical idea | Notes |
|-----------------|--------------|--------|
| Setup | e.g. **20%** of what the doctor actually paid after discount | When setup payment is confirmed |
| Monthly | e.g. **10%** of monthly fee forever (while referred tenant stays active) | Confirmed after doctor’s monthly payment |

**Not commissionable:** SMS credit packs.

**Your tools:**
- Partner panel: `/partner`
- Share referral link: `/?ref=YOURCODE`
- Optional discount codes: `/?code=CODE`
- Referral and discount ride into the WhatsApp message so credit is not lost when the doctor chats sales directly

**Payout flow (plain English):**
1. Doctor pays setup/monthly (bKash/bank)
2. Super Admin marks payment confirmed → your commission becomes **owed**
3. Super Admin pays you out → status **paid**

Keep your own bKash / payout details ready. Do not promise same-day payouts unless ops agrees.

### When a doctor asks for a feature or module (meeting, phone, or SMS)

If they ask for something specific — a module we already sell, or a new idea — **do not invent a price or a promise on the spot.**

1. **Write it down** (what they want, in their words) and send it to central / product.
2. We will **try our best to include it for free, as soon as we can**, when it fits the product and our capacity.
3. We will **tell them clearly whether it is possible or not** — no silent “maybe forever.”
4. If it is **outside our budget** (big custom work, new systems, long build), we **may charge an extra fee** — and we will **tell them the fee before any paid work starts**. Never surprise them after the fact.

**What you may say on the call:**  
“We’ll check with the team. If we can fold it into ChamberQ, we’ll try to include it at no extra charge and tell you how soon. If it’s a bigger custom job, we’ll say so and quote any extra fee before we start.”

**What you must not say:**  
“Yes, next week, free” — unless central has already confirmed that.

## 7. Product walkthrough (talking points + demo order)

Use this order on calls or in video:

1. **Chamber page** — Patients land on the doctor’s branded page and tap Book.
2. **Pick a session** — Day + session; seats left are clear.
3. **Confirm details** — Name + phone. Serial locked. **Pay at the chamber.**
4. **Serial ticket** — Place in line; shareable on WhatsApp.
5. **Live queue** — Waiting-room screen calls the next serial. Fair and calm.
6. **Doctor’s day list** — Who is booked, waiting, and next.

**Staff side (mention briefly):**
- Live Queue Control: Call → Patient arrived → Complete
- Walk-ins still welcome (staff can add them)
- Outdoor TV / screen for the waiting room
- Operational reports for end-of-day / week / month
- Staff can edit site text/photos; admin owns structure

**Roles (keep simple):**
- **Admin** — full control
- **Doctor** — ops + queue
- **Staff** — content text/image + queue

- “Patients book a serial online and pay at the chamber — like today.”

- “Fewer phone interruptions. Shorter waits. Better word of mouth.”

### Don’t say
- “Online payment / bKash deposit required to book” (false for v1)
- “Free SMS every month” (false — prepaid credits only)
- “Self-serve signup in 2 minutes” (we do done-with-you)

- Heavy tech jargon (API, multi-tenant, Filament, etc.)

### Headline themes that match the product
- Give patients their time back
- A calmer chamber
- Online serial + live queue
- Built for Bangladesh solo doctors first

## 9. Objection handling (cheat sheet)

| Objection | Answer |
|-----------|--------|
| “My patients aren’t tech-savvy.” | Booking is a few taps. Ticket shares on WhatsApp. Staff can still book walk-ins. |
| “What about walk-ins?” | Still welcome. Online serials fill seats first; remaining seats take walk-ins as usual. |
| “I’m not technical.” | We set up page, sessions, and branding with you. You don’t need to be technical. |
| “Is it Bangla?” | Patients can use Bangla when enabled. Sales chat can be in Bangla too. |
| “Do they get SMS?” | Yes, when the chamber has SMS credits (~৳0.50 each). WhatsApp ticket share is free. |
| “Can I stop anytime?” | Yes — monthly after setup. Message us to pause. |
| “I sit at 3 different chambers.” | Solo covers **up to 5 locations**. Perfect for that. |
| “I need online payment first.” | v1 is pay-at-chamber on purpose — less friction, matches desk collection. Online pay is later-stage. |
| “Why no free trial self-signup?” | We onboard personally so the chamber looks good from day one and support stays sane. |

## 12. What “good” looks like after go-live

A successful Solo client usually:
- Has Book CTA working on their page
- Runs Live Queue on session days (screen + call next)
- Sees fewer mid-consult phone interruptions
- Patients share tickets on WhatsApp
- Staff still handles walk-ins smoothly
- Pays monthly on time; tops up SMS when they want confirmations

If they only have a pretty website but never run the queue, the wait-time promise will not land — coach them to use the queue on busy days.
