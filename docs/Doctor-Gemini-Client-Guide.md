**For:** Doctors and clinics using (or considering) SoloDoc  
**Product:** SoloDoc

This guide explains what you get, how your patients use it, how your chamber day runs, and what it costs — in plain language. No technical background needed.

---

## 1. What SoloDoc is

SoloDoc is your **digital front desk** for chamber practice.

It gives you:
1. A **branded webpage** for your patients (your name, photo, services, FAQs, etc.)
2. **Online serial booking** — patients pick a day and session on their phone
3. A **serial ticket** they can save or share on WhatsApp
4. A **live waiting-room queue** (TV/screen + staff control) so calling patients stays fair and calm
5. A simple **admin panel** for you and your staff

**Important:** Patients **book online**, then **pay at the chamber** — same as your usual desk collection. There is no online payment gateway for the visit fee in this version.

**Real-life picture:**  
Like a bank token system, but on the patient’s phone. They take a number before they arrive, see roughly when to come, and your waiting room calls people in order instead of everyone crowding the door.

## 4. How patients use it (step by step)

Imagine a patient named Rafi who wants to see you next Tuesday.

1. **Opens your page**  
   Example: `doctorgemini.com/yourname` (or your own domain later, if you add one).

2. **Taps Book**  
   Chooses the day and the session (for example “Evening chamber, 5–8 pm”).

3. **Enters name and phone**  
   Serial is locked for that session. **No online payment.**

4. **Gets a ticket**  
   Shows his place in line. He can share it on WhatsApp with family.

5. **Comes to the chamber**  
   Pays at the desk as usual. Watches the waiting-room screen (or his ticket) to know when he’s next.

6. **If he forgets the link**  
   He can open your **portal**, enter his phone number, and find his booking again.

**Walk-ins still welcome.** Online serials fill seats first; remaining seats can take walk-ins the way you already do. Your staff can also book someone who walked in.

## 5. How your chamber day runs

This is the part that makes waiting feel fair.

### Before the session
- Your schedule/sessions are already set (we help you do this at setup)
- Patients who booked online already have serials

### During the session (staff or you)
1. Open **Live Queue Control** in your admin panel
2. **Start** today’s session
3. **Call** the next patient (screen + chime and/or spoken “Calling number…”)
4. When they arrive at the door / room, mark **Patient arrived**
5. When the consult finishes, mark **Complete**
6. Repeat

### Waiting-room screen
Put a simple browser page on a TV or tablet in the waiting area. Patients see who is being called — less shouting, less door crowding.

Tap once on the screen to enable sound. You can choose in Branding settings: **chime only**, **voice only**, or **chime + voice**. Voice plays a clear pre-recorded “Number twelve” (like a bank token machine), not the computer’s spooky speech engine.

### End of day / week
Open **Operational Reports** to see how many bookings completed, who is still in queue, and what needs attention. Useful for judging busy days without guessing.

## 9. Common questions

**Will this work if my patients aren’t tech-savvy?**  
Yes for most chambers. Booking is a few taps. Most people already use WhatsApp — the ticket shares there. Staff can still book walk-ins.

**What about walk-ins?**  
Still welcome. Online serials don’t ban walk-ins; they organize the line.

**Who sets it up?**  
We do — with you. You don’t need to be technical.

**Do patients pay online?**  
No. They pay at the chamber.

**Do patients get SMS?**  
When your SMS wallet has credits. Otherwise booking still works; they can use the ticket link / WhatsApp.

**Can I practice at more than one chamber?**  
Yes on Solo — **up to 5 locations**. Clinic is for larger multi-doctor setups.

**Can I stop anytime?**  
Yes. It’s monthly after setup. Message us if you need to pause.

**Will my phone still ring all day?**  
Many doctors see fewer “what’s my serial?” calls once patients have a ticket and a clearer wait. You still control how strongly you push online booking vs walk-in habits.

## 10. Tips to get the full benefit

1. **Use the live queue on busy days** — a pretty website alone won’t shorten waits; calling serials in order will.
2. **Share your booking link** — put it on your Facebook page, visiting card, and WhatsApp status.
3. **Ask staff to book walk-ins into the system** — so the screen and day list stay honest.
4. **Top up SMS** if you want automatic confirmations; otherwise rely on the ticket + WhatsApp share.
5. **Keep session seats realistic** — if you always overrun, shorten the published window or seats so the “come around” time stays trustworthy.
