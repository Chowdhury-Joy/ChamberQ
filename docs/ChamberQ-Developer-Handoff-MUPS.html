For this tenant, ChamberQ is the whole MUPS floor:

  • The public website patients expect (relief without surgery, book a serial, pay at the chamber).
  • The day list and live queue the receptionist runs while Dr. Moin is in a room.
  • The waiting-room TV for every live room in that building.
  • The prescription pad, cash khata, referring-GP ledger, and a small HR roster.
Spin this clinic up: php artisan db:seed --class=MupsSeeder. That creates tenant mups, both branches, rooms, website, fees, GPs, HR, and today’s fake patients. To refresh only the fake day list, run php artisan db:seed --class=MupsDemoSeeder. Seeders refuse APP_ENV=production and never overwrite an existing user’s password.

Queue settings (Tenant Admin → Settings → Branding)

  • Who runs the queue: Staff. Desk taps Call next. Doctor stays on Consult Screen.
  • ETA: Live average of today’s finished consults — not a guess from the timetable.
  • Announce: Chime + voice, locale Bangla. The TV says the serial the waiting room actually speaks.
  • Call timeout: 15 seconds. First N patients: 3, asked 15 minutes early.

On Dr. Moin’s card, every notify stage is on for SMS and WhatsApp (booking, doctor late, cancellation, prescription, follow-up). SMS only leaves the building when the server’s SMS driver is a real gateway. Locally it writes to the log.

Pay stays at the desk. There is no bKash checkout on the website — that is an owner decision, not a missing feature.

5 · Patient journey

From Google to a paperless ticket

  1. Land on the website. Hero: “Relief without surgery.” Book a serial; pay at the chamber.
  2. Book. The wizard only lists Consultation sittings. A patient cannot self-book intervention or counseling. Name + BD mobile. No account required. With one doctor, that step is skipped. With two, patients pick the doctor first (or arrive pre-filled from a profile Book button).
  3. Ticket. UUID link (the secret). Sitting window always. Now-serving and come-around because live queue is on. Voucher number prints when the till assigned one (not on free counseling rows).
  4. Waiting room. TV for that branch: All rooms TV for Panchlaish or Uttara. Idle rooms stay off the board so tiles stay large. The calling room inverts to light tile / dark numerals.
  5. Portal (optional). Phone lookup, or ChamberQ /me OTP locker.

Consultation sittings: cap 20 + 6 walk-in overflow. Intervention: cap 12, desk-only (no public overflow). Counseling: cap 40, desk-created after a procedure.

6 · Desk day

Reception from open to close

Think of Daily Roster as the paper register, Live Queue Control as the microphone, and the TV as the board in the hall.

  1. Morning. Open Daily Roster. Today’s Consultation / Intervention / Counseling rows are already there if the seeder ran. Walk-ins: add a person onto today’s Consultation sitting. Intervention walk-ins are desk-only.
  2. Start the sitting on Live Queue Control (or Start in Live Queue from the roster). Staff is the queue runner.
  3. Call next. TV speaks. Ticket updates. If the line drops, Call next still works on this computer; it uploads when the line returns. A second Call next while someone is still called does nothing — the doorway is occupied.
  4. Patient arrived → they are in the chamber. Doctor’s Consult Screen follows that person.
  5. Collect fee: pick catalogue chip, type cash ৳ and online ৳, optional referring GP. Discount and clinic/doctor split compute. Counseling hides Collect fee (free).
  6. Procedure done on an intervention row → Send to counseling (same day, no SMS, no voucher).
  7. Missed procedures (Operations): listed for OT but did not complete — WhatsApp + move. No auto-cancel.
  8. Mark Late if Dr. Moin is stuck in traffic — patients get the delay SMS because doctor-late notify is on.
  9. End session names who will be cancelled and offers WhatsApp links.

All rooms TV: one bookmark per branch. Panchlaish’s TV must not show Uttara’s rooms. Bookmark the chamber URL, not a single sitting. The next page is two mixed-room patients — consultation, OT, counseling — on one Saturday board.

9 · More than one doctor

When a fellow or associate joins

Today Dr. Moin is the only name on the door. Think of adding a second doctor like hiring a second chef: same restaurant, same hall TV, but each chef needs their own station, their own tickets, and their own pad. Do not copy Moin’s sitting onto the new person and hope the queue sorts it out.

What is already true on this tenant

  • Admin can add unlimited doctor profiles (Website → Doctors). Staff cannot edit BM&DC, fees, or the paper-Rx toggle.
  • A doctor profile and a login are two things. Pair them with Login account on the Doctors form. One login, one profile. A visiting doctor may have a public card and no login.
  • Never guess the pairing. The wrong pair prints the wrong name and registration number on a prescription.

How to set the second person up

  1. Create the doctor profile: name, qualifications, BM&DC, photo, Show on website, fees, notify channels, whether staff may type their paper Rx.
  2. Create a doctor login (or pair an existing one). Example shape: associate@mups.local — not a second use of doctor@mups.local.
  3. Give them their own sittings on Schedule Sessions: branch + weekday + room type + clock. A sitting always belongs to one doctor. If the associate only sees consultations in Uttara on Tuesdays, that Consultation row is theirs; Moin’s Intervention row stays Moin’s.
  4. Counseling still follows the procedure doctor’s chamber/day. Send to counseling looks for a counseling sitting for that doctor in that building. If the associate will finish procedures, they need a counseling sitting too, or the button hides.
Two realistic MUPS shapes: (1) Same building, two rooms — Moin in Intervention, associate in Consultation, one waiting-room TV. (2) Same brand, different days — associate covers a Panchlaish afternoon Moin used to fly for. Days can overlap once there are two people; the “never the same city” rule was for one body, not a software lock.

What patients and desk then see

  • Book: Pick doctor appears. Dates and Consultation sittings are only that doctor’s. Intervention and counseling stay off the public wizard.
  • Website: Doctors page lists both. Homepage grid is cards, not the single founder poster. Tick Show on website off for a fellow who should not be bookable from Google.
  • Desk: Daily Roster and Live Queue Control list every sitting today. The receptionist picks the room the way they pick a counter. Walk-ins must land on the right doctor’s Consultation sitting — the wrong sitting is the wrong pad and the wrong fee split.
  • TV: All rooms TV is still per building. If both doctors are live in Panchlaish, both rooms can appear (up to four tiles). Uttara’s TV must not show Panchlaish. Idle rooms stay off the board.
  • Money: The fee catalogue is clinic-wide (Consultation ৳1,000, epidural ৳8,000…). House share vs doctor share is on the chip, not a second price list per name. If an associate charges differently, add a catalogue row or use that doctor’s extra fees — do not silently reuse Moin’s consultation chip and hope.
  • Prescription: The booking’s sitting doctor wins. If Moin covers the associate’s list, the printed pad is still the sitting’s name. My medicines and packs stay on the signed-in doctor. Staff paper entry follows the sitting doctor’s toggle, not whoever is at the desk.
Consult Screen today follows the most recently started live sitting in the whole MUPS tenant — it does not pin to “whoever just logged in.” Two doctors in two rooms at once can see each other’s in-chamber patient. Until that is split, run one live consult at a time, or have desk Call next only on the room whose doctor is actually at that computer. Live Queue Control is already per sitting; that is the safe board for two rooms.

10 · What not to “fix”

Locks, go-live, and a five-minute check

Do not shorten session idle timeout unless the owner says change session expiry or restore session timeout. Desk machines stay signed in on purpose.

Do not add online payment on the MUPS website unless the owner asks.

Do not restore a Dhanmondi / Epic Healthcare third branch.

Before this database faces real patients on a public domain, run:

php artisan app:production-check --strict

It will refuse a laptop that still has debug on, SQLite, localhost as APP_URL, and SMS/mail writing to log files. That is the remaining go-live list for this clinic — not another toggle.

Quick test after you pull

  1. Seed MUPS. Open /mups/ — homepage loads, nav has Centres, not a 500.
  2. Tenant admin → Chambers = 2. Schedule = Intervention / Consultation / Counseling on each branch day.
  3. /mups/book offers Consultation only.
  4. /mups/centres shows Panchlaish and Uttara, not Epic Healthcare.
  5. Live Queue Control → All rooms TV for today’s branch.
  6. If you add a second doctor: wizard shows Pick doctor; new sittings are theirs; Consult Screen still follows the latest live sitting — confirm before two rooms run at once.

Tests that pin this clinic: php artisan test --filter=MupsWebsiteTest and MupsDemoSeederTest. Production database engine is MySQL; green on SQLite is not the same as green on MySQL.
