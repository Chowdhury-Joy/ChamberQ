0 · Picture this first

What you are looking at

Like a small hospital with one specialist who flies between two cities. Software has to know which building he is in today, which room is calling, and that a patient booked online for a visit cannot accidentally land on the OT list.

ChamberQ here is not a generic “clinic app.” For MUPS it is:

  • The public website patients already expect (relief without surgery, book a serial, pay at the chamber).
  • The day list and live queue the receptionist runs while Dr. Moin is in a room.
  • The waiting-room TV that can show every live room in that building.
  • The prescription pad, cash khata, referring-GP ledger, and a small HR roster.
Seed this clinic: php artisan db:seed --class=MupsSeeder. That is the same as Super Admin opening the tenant and ticking every product box, then filling Branding queue settings. Re-fill today’s fake patients with php artisan db:seed --class=MupsDemoSeeder only.

Queue branding (Tenant Admin → Settings → Branding)

These are the knobs the receptionist would set once, like choosing the doorbell sound:

  • Who runs the queue: Staff (desk Call next; doctor stays in Consult Screen).
  • ETA model: Live average (today’s finished consults, not a guess from the timetable).
  • Announce: Chime + voice, locale Bangla — the TV says the serial in the language the waiting room actually speaks.
  • Call timeout: 15 seconds. First N patients: 3, asked 15 minutes early.

On the doctor card, every notify stage is on for both SMS and WhatsApp (booking, doctor late, cancellation, prescription, follow-up). SMS still only leaves the building when the server’s SMS driver is a real gateway — locally it writes to the log.

4 · Patient journey

From Google to a paperless ticket

  1. Land on the website. Hero: “Relief without surgery.” Book a serial; pay at the chamber. Language switcher EN | BN is on.
  2. Book. The wizard only lists Visit sittings. A patient cannot self-book the OT (intervention) or counseling list. Name + BD mobile, no account required.
  3. Ticket. UUID link (the secret). Shows sitting window always. With Live queue on, it also shows now-serving and come-around. Voucher number prints when Stations assigned one (not on free counseling rows).
  4. Waiting room. TV at that branch: All rooms TV for Panchlaish or Uttara. Idle rooms stay off the board so tiles stay large. Calling room inverts to light tile / dark numerals.
  5. Portal (optional). Phone lookup, or ChamberQ /me OTP locker across clinics.

Pay stays at the desk. There is no bKash checkout on the website — that is an owner decision, not a missing feature.

5 · Desk day (queue)

Reception from open to close

Think of Daily Roster as the paper register, Live Queue Control as the microphone, and the TV as the board in the hall.

  1. Morning. Open Daily Roster. Today’s Visit / Intervention / Counseling rows are already there if the seeder ran. Walk-ins: add a person onto today’s Visit sitting (public kind). Intervention walk-ins are desk-only.
  2. Start the sitting on Live Queue Control (or Start in Live Queue from the roster). Staff is the queue runner.
  3. Call next. TV speaks. Ticket updates. If the line drops, Call next still works on this computer; it uploads when the line returns. A second Call next while someone is still called does nothing — the doorway is occupied.
  4. Patient arrived → they are in the chamber. Doctor’s Consult Screen follows that person.
  5. Collect fee (Stations form): pick catalogue chip (Visit new / Follow-up / Epidural / RFA / PRP…), type cash ৳ and online ৳, optional referring GP. Discount and clinic/doctor split compute. Counseling and leftover consult kinds hide Collect fee (free).
  6. Procedure done on an intervention row → Send to counseling (same day, no SMS, no voucher).
  7. Missed procedures (Operations): people who were listed for OT but did not complete — WhatsApp + move. No auto-cancel.
  8. Mark Late if Dr. Moin is stuck in traffic — patients get the delay SMS because doctor-late notify is on.
  9. End session names who will be cancelled and offers WhatsApp links.

All rooms TV: one bookmark per branch. Panchlaish’s TV must not show Uttara’s rooms. Bookmark the chamber URL, not a single sitting.

6 · Doctor, money, people

Consult, cash, referrals, HR

Consult Screen

Follows the in-chamber patient. Write prescription (staff may also enter a paper Rx later — that toggle is on for Dr. Moin). Complete visit, then Call next is a separate tap so the prescription can be handed over while the patient is still in the room.

Cashbook

Operations → Cashbook. Patient fees from Collect fee; expenses (rent, salary, referral payout). Mixed cash + online is supported. Categories are under Operations → Cash categories.

Referrals

Seeded GPs: Dr. Karim (Panchlaish) and Dr. Sultana (Uttara ortho). At Collect fee, pick “Referred by.” End of month: Referral ledger → Mark selected as paid.

HR

Three people: desk (linked to staff@mups.local), procedure nurse Panchlaish, procedure nurse Uttara. Attendance / leave / payroll live under the HR nav. Paying salary writes a cashbook expense in the same breath as the payroll row — so a double-click cannot leave an orphan expense.

Offline

Pack bag (Rx) and queue snapshot work on this computer if the ISP dies. There is no separate queue worker to install — that is deliberate for a chamber laptop.

7 · What not to “fix”

Locks and production reality

Do not shorten session idle timeout unless the owner says change session expiry or restore session timeout. Desk machines stay signed in on purpose.

Do not restyle the solo patient homepage. MUPS uses the clinic Clireo site, which is fair game; the locked files are resources/views/tenant/solo/**.

Before this database faces real patients on a public domain, run:

php artisan app:production-check --strict

It will refuse a laptop that still has debug on, SQLite, localhost as APP_URL, and SMS/mail writing to log files. That is the remaining “go live” list — not another feature toggle in Super Admin.

Quick test after you pull

  1. Seed MUPS. Open /mups/ — homepage loads, nav has Centres, not a 500.
  2. Open Super Admin tenant mups — Website, Queue, Rx, Stations, Referrals, HR all ticked.
  3. Tenant admin → Chambers = 2. Schedule = Intervention / Visit / Counseling on each branch day.
  4. /mups/book offers Visit only.
  5. Live Queue Control → All rooms TV for today’s branch.

Tests that pin this clinic: php artisan test --filter=MupsWebsiteTest and MupsDemoSeederTest.
